Most people don't lose their stuff. They just walk out the door without it. ReadyCheck learns what you need, checks whether you have it, and alerts you before you leave without it.

The problem

Forgetting stuff is normal. Turning around for it shouldn't be.

From the ReadyCheck customer survey, original analysis of 44 responses.

47.7% forget something important at least once a week
79.6% say an automatic reminder system would be at least somewhat helpful
59.1% go to three or more places on a typical weekday

What people worried about

We asked what would hold people back. These are the problems we are designing around: price, setup and tags.

High purchase price59.1%
Complicated setup50%
Attaching tags to belongings40.9%
How it works

Three parts. One simple job.

01

Tag it.

Attach small wireless tags to the things you carry most, like keys, backpacks, laptops, chargers and gym bags.

  • Only tag what you want checked
  • Add or remove items in the app
02

It learns.

For the first 2 to 3 weeks, ReadyCheck’s AI learns which items leave with you on which days.

  • Builds a routine for each day
  • Gets more personal over time
03

It checks.

When you head out, the sensor by your door looks for the day’s items. Missing one? Your phone gets an alert.

  • Checks before you leave, not after
  • Override any reminder when plans change
